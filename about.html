Our Heritage

Built on Uncompromising Quality & Precision Logistics

Founded in 1998 to solve industrial fastener stockouts, Titan has grown into a master distribution network supplying over 1.8 million metric tonnes of certified hardware.

We operate three state-of-the-art master fulfillment centers equipped with automated optical sorting systems, accredited mechanical testing labs, and computer-controlled pallet staging docks. Whether securing critical bridge piers or high-pressure refinery piping, our hardware is trusted where failure is never an option.
